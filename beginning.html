Where Every Trail Begins

A New Adventure

Hiking is one of my favourite hobbies because every trail offers a different adventure. I enjoy leaving the noise of everyday life behind and entering a world filled with mountains, trees, rivers and wildlife. When I start a hike, I never know exactly what I will discover.

I always take my camera or cellphone with me. Photography helps me capture the small and large wonders that I find along the way. Some people walk quickly to reach the end of the trail, but I prefer to take my time and observe everything around me.
