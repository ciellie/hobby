Discoveries Along the Trail

From Tiny Spiders to Large Animals

Some of the most interesting things are also the easiest to miss. A tiny spider may be sitting on a leaf beside the path, building a delicate web between two branches. I enjoy stopping to photograph the patterns in the web and the small drops of water shining on it.

Further along the trail, I may find colourful insects, unusual flowers, animal tracks or birds resting in the trees. Each photograph becomes part of the story of my journey.

Sometimes the trail offers a much bigger surprise. A distant movement between the trees may turn out to be a leopard quietly wandering away. Seeing an animal like that in the wild is exciting, but I always keep a safe distance and avoid disturbing it.

Wildlife Safety

Wild animals should always be respected. I never approach or feed them. I remain calm, keep my distance and use the zoom on my camera when taking photographs.
